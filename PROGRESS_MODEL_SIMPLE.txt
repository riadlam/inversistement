OWNER SCHEDULE TIMELINE (DEMO SCREEN) — SHORT NOTE
==================================================

WHAT THE GREEN BAR IS
---------------------
• Start = commencement order date (execution start).
• End   = start + “project_duration_months” from the project owner (e.g. 24 months = 2 years).
• The fill shows how much of THAT time has passed (linear). So after 1 year of a 2-year window → about 50%.

QUARTER TICKS
-------------
• Every 3 months along the same window we draw a small vertical tick (Q1, Q2, …).
• On a 2-year plan there are 8 quarter steps; each step is the same fraction of the bar (~12.5% of the full length).

EVENT DOTS
----------
• Maturation, documents, audit entries, etc. are placed on the bar by their real dates.

BIG PERCENT IN THE CORNER (“Schedule elapsed”)
-----------------------------------------------
• Same as the bar: calendar only. It is NOT calculated from money (AE/CP) or SPU.

PHYSICAL / FINANCIAL PROGRESS
-----------------------------
• Not mixed into this timeline block. Physical % and money (AE/CP) appear in their own cards lower on the page.

VALIDATION
----------
• Official checks stay with the project owner / control; this is a demo visual.
